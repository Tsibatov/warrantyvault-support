PetDose: Support

Free access

All PetDose features are free, without subscriptions or in-app purchases. There is no paid limit on active records.

Records and documents

The app stores pets, medication instructions, schedules, given or skipped dose events, caregiver labels, refills and documents. Document recognition runs on your device. Review recognized text before saving it.

Storage and reminders

This version stores data locally; cross-device synchronization is not enabled. Allow notifications in iOS Settings for reminders. Delivery depends on system settings and Focus modes.

Export and deletion

Export records from the app settings. Exported copies can contain personal data. Deleting records in the app does not delete previously exported copies.

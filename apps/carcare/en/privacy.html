CarCare: Privacy policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026.

App data

CarCare stores data you enter on your device: vehicles, mileage, maintenance schedules, repairs, costs and service documents. Automatic iCloud synchronization is not enabled in this version. Device backups are managed separately through Apple.

Recognition and notifications

Document recognition runs locally. Documents are not sent to an external OCR service. Reminders are scheduled on your device with your permission; their text may appear on the Lock Screen.

Purchases and analytics

This version has no in-app purchases, advertising or analytics sent to the publisher. It does not request paid-access entitlements.

Exports, retention and deletion

Records remain until you delete them. Export and the system share sheet send selected data to the destination you choose. Exported copies and backups must be managed separately. Emailing support shares the message and attachments with the publisher. Support messages are retained while needed to respond and meet applicable obligations.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts these pages. GitHub processes requests and IP addresses to operate and protect its service under its privacy statement. The publisher uses no analytics or advertising trackers on these pages. Visiting the website does not provide access to app records.

How can we help?

A few practical steps for keeping your receipts and purchase details together.

A receipt was not read correctly

Place the receipt flat in good light and include the full page. Avoid glare, shadows and motion blur. You can scan again, import a clearer photo or PDF, or enter the details manually. Review the suggested merchant, date, currency and product amount before saving.

A receipt may not state the warranty or return period. Add those dates from the retailer's or manufacturer's information instead of relying on an inferred policy.

Completely free access

All features are free, with unlimited active purchases. Scanning, reminders, iCloud sync and export do not require a subscription or an in-app purchase.

Reminders are not appearing

Check that the purchase has a return or warranty date and that reminders are enabled for it. Then allow notifications for WarrantyVault in iOS Settings. Focus modes and notification settings can affect how alerts appear. Open the app to review upcoming dates; notification delivery is controlled by iOS.

A purchase has not appeared on another device

Use the same iCloud account on both devices, check available iCloud storage and connectivity, and review the iCloud status in the app's Settings. Sync can take time, especially for documents. Local purchases remain usable while a device is offline.

Exporting or deleting your records

Open Settings → Export My Data to save a copy of your vault, or Export CSV for an item summary. Individual purchases can also be shared from their details. Exports may include personal information from your receipts.

You can delete an individual purchase or use Settings → Delete All Data. Deletion propagates through iCloud when sync is available. Copies you previously exported or saved in backups are managed separately. Read the privacy policy for details.

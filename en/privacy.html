Privacy policy

Publisher: Timur Sibatov
Effective date: October 25, 2026

WarrantyVault keeps the purchase records you choose to save. Receipt recognition takes place on your device.

Information in your vault

You can save product and merchant names, purchase dates and amounts, serial and model numbers, return and warranty dates, notes, tags, folders, repair history, recognized receipt text, photos and documents. Receipts and documents may contain personal information such as a name, address or payment details.

These records are stored locally on your device. The app has no separate user-account database or custom backend. It does not contain advertising trackers or a third-party analytics service, and does not automatically send usage events to the publisher.

Receipt recognition and permissions

WarrantyVault uses Apple's Vision framework to recognize receipt text on your device. It does not send receipt images or recognized text to a third-party OCR service. Recognition may be inaccurate; you can review and edit the result.

The camera is used when you choose to scan a document. The system photo and file pickers let you select documents to import. You can enter a purchase manually without camera access.

iCloud synchronization

When iCloud is configured and available, WarrantyVault uses Apple's private CloudKit database to synchronize records and attachments between your Apple devices. Apple operates iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy. Synchronization may be delayed while offline, signed out, low on storage or otherwise restricted; local records remain usable.

This policy makes no promise of end-to-end encryption. Details of Apple's services are available in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Purchases and reminders

The current free version does not offer in-app purchases or request StoreKit purchase entitlements. WarrantyVault does not receive payment-card details or operate a payment server.

Reminders are scheduled locally with your permission. Notification text may include a product name and deadline, which can appear on your Lock Screen depending on your iOS settings. You can change reminders in the app and notification permissions or previews in iOS Settings.

Exports and diagnostics

You can export records from Settings and share records or documents through the system share sheet. You choose the destination. Exported copies leave the app's control; manage those copies in the apps or services where you saved them.

Diagnostics are created only when you select Export Diagnostics. They include the app and OS version, aggregate record counts, notification permission status and access status (free in the current version). They exclude receipt text, product and merchant names, amounts, serial numbers, documents and device or account identifiers. Diagnostics are not uploaded automatically. Temporary diagnostic files may be replaced by a later export or removed by the operating system.

Retention and deletion

Your vault records remain until you delete them. You can delete individual records or use Delete All Data in Settings. The app removes its stored records and associated data and cancels their reminders. A shared receipt can remain while another purchase still uses it.

CloudKit propagates deletions when synchronization is available. Offline devices may take time to receive changes. Deleting the app alone does not necessarily delete iCloud copies, previously shared exports or copies in system backups. Apple controls its service and backup retention; review your Apple storage settings for those copies.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact support, the publisher receives the contact information and content you choose to send, such as your message, screenshots or an exported diagnostics file. This information is used to respond to your request. Start with a description of the issue; receipt images and payment details are not required to open a support request.

Support messages and attachments are kept for as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. They are deleted when they are no longer needed for those purposes. You can ask the publisher to delete information you have sent to support.

For support, privacy questions or requests relating to information you have sent to the publisher, contact: tsibatov@yahoo.com. You can manage the records in your vault directly using the app's export and deletion controls.

This website and policy updates

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms or externally loaded fonts added by the publisher.

GitHub processes requests to serve the website and logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub controls its hosting-service processing and retention under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The publisher does not use these pages to track activity in the app. Visiting this website does not give GitHub access to the purchase records in your vault.

If the app's data handling changes, this page and the relevant App Store disclosures will be updated. The effective date above identifies the policy in force.
